Integration points

  1. IG bio link plus podcast description plus YouTube description go to scorecard. Single CTA, single link. The scorecard routes to the right back-end at the score-output step.
  2. @astroflipping bio link goes to Astro Flipping enrolment landing page directly. Brand-specific surface for buyers already converted on intent.
  3. Scorecard goes to email plus segmentation tag. Tag fires on completion: experience tier plus lead source plus dispo channel plus primary bottleneck plus target volume plus program-intent band.
  4. Email nurture goes to the program-specific application or VSL landing. Welcome flow (14-day) plus 7-touch pre-enrolment sequence segmented by which scorecard band the buyer is tagged into.
  5. Application Typeform goes to Stripe checkout (Astro Flipping mentorship) or to Calendly (Housing Alchemy, Energy of Money discovery calls). Routing by program.
  6. Post-purchase sequence runs by program. Astro Flipping buyers get the seven-module onboarding plus Elite Facebook Group invite plus monthly coaching call calendar. Astro Accelerator buyers get the seven-module onboarding plus cross-sell to Astro Flipping mentorship at week 4. Housing Alchemy and Energy of Money buyers get their program-specific onboarding.
  7. Cross-brand cross-sell at the email level. Astro Accelerator buyers see a Housing Alchemy intro sequence at month 2. Astro Flipping buyers see an Energy of Money intro sequence at month 3. Housing Alchemy buyers see an Astro Accelerator intro sequence at month 2. All cross-sells are non-coercive and segmentation-aware.

The "no setter" model for Astro Accelerator and Astro Flipping

Astro Accelerator at the intro tier stays direct-to-Stripe. The VSL plus the scorecard routing handles the qualification. The buyer arrives at Stripe pre-qualified.

Astro Flipping upper-tier mentorship gets an application Typeform plus a Stripe checkout. The application is 4 minutes. Submission triggers Stripe. No setter call. No phone screen. The application's purpose is segmentation and qualification, not gatekeeping.

Housing Alchemy and Energy of Money get a Calendly discovery call layer because the program shape is higher-touch and longer-arc, and the buyer typically wants a human conversation before enrolment.

This is the time-cost reduction for Jamil vs running every applicant through a personal call. The install gives Jamil a 4-minute application Typeform for Astro Flipping enrolment review (asynchronous, takes 5 minutes per applicant), plus a Calendly call layer for the higher-touch programs.
