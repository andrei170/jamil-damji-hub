Voice rules

Speak in

  • Specific deal mechanics, specific market types, specific dispo channels, specific lead sources
  • The vocabulary of the wholesaling trade - "comp," "triangulation," "spread," "wholesale," "creative finance," "structure," "dispo," "cash buyer," "JV partner," "off-market," "seller," "PSA," "earnest," "closing" - never softened or translated
  • Decision-tree language ("if you have zero deals, the bottleneck is lead source; if you have 1-3 deals, the bottleneck is seller conversion; if you have 4-10 deals and the pace stalled, the bottleneck is dispo or capital")
  • First-person operator voice ("I have run this since 2002," "we close X deals a month at KeyGlee," "the mentorship walks the same playbook the podcast previews")
  • The since-2002 framing and the KeyGlee scale as observable fact, not as flex
  • The Wholesale Hotline cold-call demo cadence where it lands naturally; the @jamildamji YouTube long-form vocabulary where it lands naturally

Do not speak in

  • Hedge-fund-analyst register or academic-finance vocabulary
  • Life-coach vagueness or self-development-influencer generality
  • Generic agency-coach influencer language as a hero headline
  • Em dashes (commas, periods, or "-" only)
  • Triple-adjective lists or "not just X, but Y" structures
  • Wholesaling-income guarantees or deal-volume promises - the mentorship teaches the craft, it does not promise specific revenue outcomes
  • Anything that reads as competing with KeyGlee - the mentorship teaches the craft, KeyGlee is the separate franchise business

Brand pillars

The four content pillars that underwrite every piece of copy across the funnel:

  1. The seven modules are the spine. Lead generation, seller conversion, dispo, capital, structure, JV, scaling. Every piece of content maps to one of the seven. Every Astro Flipping member walks all seven.
  2. The mentorship is the install version of the podcast. Wholesale Hotline sells the framework via cold-call demos. The mentorship walks the live operation across the seven modules with the monthly coaching call and the Elite Facebook Group.
  3. 20+ years in real estate, KeyGlee 35+ metros, since 2002. Stated as observable fact, never as flex. Authority comes from the operating track record at scale, not from the headline.
  4. The mentorship teaches the craft. KeyGlee is the separate franchise business. Astro Flipping members who want a full franchise opportunity have the keyglee.com surface as a separate route. The mentorship does not feed KeyGlee, and KeyGlee does not feed the mentorship. Two businesses, one operator.

  • "The mentorship exists. The Accelerator exists. Housing Alchemy and Energy of Money exist. KeyGlee exists. The install connects them."

  • AG1 does not create Astro Accelerator - it already exists.
  • AG1 does not create Housing Alchemy or Energy of Money - they already exist.
  • AG1 does not touch KeyGlee - it operates as a separate franchise business.
