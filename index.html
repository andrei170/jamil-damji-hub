Questions

The Ones An Operator At Your Stage Asks

Short, direct answers. The longer ones we cover on the call.

Why would I run this with an outside operator instead of in-house?

Because the team you have is already running the business. AstroFlipping is fulfilling and selling. KeyGlee is doing dispositions across 100+ markets. The A&E commitments and content cadence are real. Adding a 90-day VSL build, a dedicated lead-magnet page, a premium Cohort tier, and a paid acquisition layer on top of an already-loaded team is how those projects either get postponed by a year or get launched at half the quality. Outside operator is faster, cleaner, and exits when production hits steady state. We hand the system to your team after the launch cycle, not before.

How much of my time does this need?

One day to film the AstroFlipping VSL. One day to film the Operator's Cohort positioning. Calendar holds for cohort delivery in Lever 02 (two days at the KeyGlee Phoenix HQ per intake). The rest is build team work plus you reviewing the copy, creative, and pricing decisions before they ship. Most operators at your stage spend under 8 hours a month during the 90-day build, and under 2 hours a month after the system is in production.

Will the Operator's Cohort cannibalise the Accelerator or the Executive tier?

No. The Cohort and the Accelerator solve different problems. Accelerator is self-paced curriculum plus monthly coaching for wholesalers earlier in the journey. The Cohort is strategic operating-room access for wholesalers who already cleared the curriculum, closed 3-5 deals, and are ready to scale to a market-level operation with KeyGlee disposition network access. Different deliverable, different audience, different buying motion. Most Cohort applicants will be Accelerator (and Executive) graduates moving up the ladder, not net-new buyers choosing Cohort over Accelerator. Where there's overlap, the Cohort is the explicit "post-Executive" tier. It expands the ladder, it doesn't compete inside it.

How does this work alongside KeyGlee?

KeyGlee is the operator brand. AstroFlipping is the education brand. This build sits on the AstroFlipping side, with KeyGlee as the structural moat for the Cohort tier (operator-tier disposition network access is the asset no other wholesaler-educator can match). The Cohort doesn't change KeyGlee operations or the KeyGlee partner model. It uses KeyGlee as the deal-room backdrop for the in-person intake at the Phoenix HQ and as the disposition partner for cohort members during the 90-day implementation.

What if my existing Executive tier already covers some of what the Cohort would?

There's overlap on some content (creative wholesaling, comp work, disposition strategy), but the deliverable is different. Executive is "we teach you the deeper methodology and give you more coaching." Cohort is "you've already proven 3-5 closed deals, now we operate at peer-room scale at the KeyGlee HQ, audit your live operation, and connect you into the operator-tier disposition network." Most Cohort attendees will have already passed through Accelerator and Executive and outgrown the curriculum. The overlap is feature, not bug. Cohort buys insider continuity for operators who don't want to start from scratch with another educator.

How quickly is there real revenue?

Lever 01 VSL on astroflipping.com: first measurable conversion lift in week 4-6 once the page ships. /wholesale-blueprint landing page email capture: measurable in week 2-3 once IG bio link rewires. Most of the year-one incremental lands in months 4-12, not month one.

What if the Cohort doesn't fill?

The Cohort is sized at 6-8 seats first, sold to existing AstroFlipping alumni (the warmest list for this offer that exists). If the first intake under-fills (say, 4 confirmed instead of 6), we don't push it into a half-empty room. We pause, sharpen the positioning and the application screen, push the date by 30-45 days, and try again with the second wave of alumni outreach plus any external warm sources we can verify. Build doesn't continue billing while the Cohort isn't running. If after a second attempt the audience isn't there at the modelled price, we restructure the tier or move the budget into Lever 03 paid acquisition.

You can stop at any Lever. After Lever 01 you own the /wholesale-blueprint landing page, the AstroFlipping VSL, and the segmented nurture as standalone lifts on your existing funnel. After Lever 02 you own the Cohort sales page, application flow, 90-day curriculum, and the first cohort revenue. After Lever 03 you own the paid acquisition system. Each Lever stands on its own as a fully shipped asset in case the rest stops being interesting to you.
