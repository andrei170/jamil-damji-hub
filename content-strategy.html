The job content has to do

The funnel infrastructure sits between the free content (Wholesale Hotline, To The Moon, @jamildamji YouTube, @jdamji / @astroflipping / @astroflippin IG, LinkedIn, TikTok) and the existing back-end offers (Astro Flipping, Astro Accelerator, Housing Alchemy, Energy of Money). Content has to do three things in 2026, in this order:

  1. Feed the scorecard. Every reel, every short, every podcast episode pushes a soft CTA to the Wholesaling Readiness Score quiz, the lead-magnet entry to the segmented email list.
  2. Pre-sell the mentorship. Specific module-depth reels and long-form videos (lead generation, seller conversion, comp and triangulation, structure, dispo, JV, scaling) seed the seven Astro Flipping modules weeks before each enrolment cycle opens.
  3. Route warm viewers to the right lane. Astro Flipping mentorship for the 1-to-10-deal operator. Astro Accelerator for the zero-to-first-deal wholesaler. Housing Alchemy for the co-living-curious operator. Energy of Money for the longer-arc buyer. KeyGlee for the franchise-curious operator (separate business, separate route).

The funnel infrastructure is the new install. The content does the marketing job for it. The existing direct-to-Stripe Astro Accelerator checkout stays as the on-ramp.
