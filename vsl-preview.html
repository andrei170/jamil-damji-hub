The 90-second preview script

Total runtime: 85-95 seconds. Shoot vertical. Phone or DSLR. Single take preferred.

Beat 1 - The hook (0:00 - 0:08)

On screen (Jamil direct to camera):

"In real estate since 2002. Co-founder of KeyGlee, national wholesaling franchise across 35-plus metros. Here is what is missing on astroflipping.com, and what I am about to install."

Production note: Hook lands the credibility (since-2002 plus KeyGlee scale) plus the curiosity (the thing missing on the enrolment page). Eight seconds total. Direct to camera, no cutaway.

Beat 2 - The diagnosis (0:08 - 0:30)

On screen (Jamil, mix of direct to camera plus 1-2 cutaway shots of him at desk with a deal sheet on screen):

"The reason most wholesalers stay stuck at zero deals, or 1-to-2 deals a month, is almost never one thing. It is one of four. Lead generation, seller conversion, dispo, capital and structure.

Every operation I have ever closed at scale, the bottleneck is one of those four. The other three are running fine."

Production note: Twenty-two seconds. Cutaways are the b-roll bank from the photoshoot. Inside: him at a desk with a comping spreadsheet or a cold-call queue on screen, no seller names visible.

Beat 3 - The fix (0:30 - 0:55)

On screen (Jamil direct to camera, kinetic):

"Astro Flipping is seven modules, walked at the pace your operation can absorb. Monthly coaching call with me, live. Elite Facebook Group, where the rest of the cohort posts the deals they are working.

Same playbook we run at KeyGlee. Same playbook the Wholesale Hotline cold-call demos preview. The mentorship is the install version of the podcast."

Production note: Twenty-five seconds. This is the offer-clarity beat. Kinetic, not lecture. Cutaways: hand on a deal-flow doc, a screen showing the seven-module map.

Beat 4 - The CTA (0:55 - 1:25)

On screen (Jamil direct to camera, calm):

"Application is a four-minute form. Submit, get reviewed, you are routed straight to enrolment. No setter call, no back and forth.

If you have listened to Wholesale Hotline and you know whether your bottleneck is lead source or dispo, you are halfway in. If you do not know which one is capping you, run the 90-second Wholesaling Readiness Score first. Link in bio.

Astro Flipping. The podcast is the pre-sell. The seven modules are the install. Link in bio."

Production note: Thirty seconds. The CTA is calm, not pushy. The brand signature line "Wholesale Hotline is the pre-sell. The seven modules are the install." anchors. Cutaways: brief shot of the enrolment landing page on a laptop, brief shot of the seven-module map.

Production notes

Equipment: - Phone (iPhone 14 or newer recommended) shooting 4K vertical, or a DSLR if Jamil already has one available - One key light or a window-light setup, no flat overhead - Lav mic (Rode Wireless GO II or similar) for audio, no on-camera mic - Off-screen teleprompter is fine; the script is short enough to memorise in 2 takes

Look: - Background should read as working wholesaling operator. Office or home office with desk plus screens plus a deal-flow board view. Phoenix or KeyGlee HQ is fine. - No "studio podcast" backdrop, no curtain-and-mic setup - Wardrobe: clean, operator-casual, button-down or polo, no heavy branded merch

Length discipline: - The preview is 90 seconds. Not 120. Not 75. Cut anything that does not land an information beat in 5 seconds. - The seven-module map at Beat 3 is the slowest beat. Up to 25 seconds is fine because that is the conversion piece. - The CTA at Beat 4 cannot run over 30 seconds.

Captions: - Burned-in captions for IG / TikTok / Shorts (80%-plus of viewers watch muted) - AG1 or Jamil's brand colour for the caption text once colour-lock happens on Day 1 of install

B-roll: - 6-8 cutaway clips from the photoshoot bank - A comping spreadsheet or a cold-call queue on a screen (anonymised, no seller names visible) - A KeyGlee buyer-list or franchise-map screenshot (anonymised, no contact info visible) - A laptop showing the seven-module Astro Flipping map - A whiteboard with the four bottlenecks written (lead gen, seller conversion, dispo, capital and structure)

  • Does not promise deal volume. Results vary, dependent on market conditions, capital, lead source, and operator inputs.
  • Does not promise KeyGlee franchise placement. KeyGlee is a separate franchise business at keyglee.com.
  • Does not replace the existing direct-to-Stripe checkout on astroflipping.com. The new VSL landing page can run as a parallel funnel page if Jamil prefers to leave the existing checkout untouched.
