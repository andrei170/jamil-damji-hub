Why the funnel infrastructure is missing (read)

A few likely reasons, ranked:

  1. Operator-saturation. Running KeyGlee at 55 franchises across 35 metros, running Astro Flipping mentorship across thousands of members historically, co-hosting Wholesale Hotline weekly, hosting To The Moon, posting on three IG handles and a YouTube channel does not leave room to build VSL recordings, scorecard logic, application Typeforms, segmented email flows, and a unified hub. The funnel infrastructure needs an install partner, not Jamil-built tech.
  2. Catalogue-as-marketing inertia. The catalogue is doing the marketing job. Cold-call demos on Wholesale Hotline pre-sell Astro Flipping. The YouTube tutorials pre-sell Astro Accelerator. The IG reels pre-sell every program at once. The next layer of conversion lift (VSL, scorecard, application gate, segmented capture) is the install that compounds what the catalogue already does. Until someone builds it, the catalogue keeps doing the marketing job at the same baseline.
  3. Three-brand operational fragmentation. Astro Flipping, Housing Alchemy, Energy of Money, KeyGlee each evolved at different times for different audiences. The funnel infrastructure that should sit across all of them never got built because each brand was launched on its own surface with its own tooling. The unified hub is the cross-brand layer that does not yet exist.

All three are install problems, not strategy problems. AG1 is built for this exact shape of install.

Wedge (this is the actual pitch)

Four pieces of funnel infrastructure that AG1 builds, mapped to specific gaps:

  1. VSL on the Astro Flipping enrolment surface. Custom 5-7 minute VSL filmed by Jamil, embedded on a rebuilt astroflipping.com sales page (or a parallel funnel page if Jamil wants to leave the existing checkout untouched). Pre-frame the seven modules, the monthly coaching call, the Elite Facebook Group. Compounds every podcast and YouTube view that lands on the enrolment page.

  2. Qualification scorecard before checkout and application. A 10-12 question "Wholesaling Readiness Score" returns a 0-100 score, a 3-line bottleneck readout, an email capture, and a routing decision. Buyers who score in the right band route to Astro Flipping mentorship. Buyers who score lower route to Astro Accelerator. Buyers who score in the co-living-curious band route to Housing Alchemy. Buyers who score in the longer-arc band route to Energy of Money. Built on ScoreApp or Typeform with intent tagging.

  3. Segmented email capture between the content and the offers. AG1 installs the ConvertKit (or GHL) email lane plus SimpleTexting SMS sequences. Welcome flow segmented by which scorecard band the buyer came in through. Pre-application nurture, post-application nurture, post-purchase onboarding. The newsletter on jdamji.com gets segmented by offer interest.

  4. Unified landing-page hub plus GHL CRM consolidation. Astro Flipping, Housing Alchemy, Energy of Money, the two podcasts, and the YouTube channel surface from a single hub. Each brand retains its distinct domain and back-end. The hub is the routing layer that captures the lead, sends the scorecard, and hands off to the right back-end. KeyGlee stays separate as the franchise business; the hub references it as authority signal, not as a feeder.

Year-1 floor on the four-piece install (illustrative, dependent on existing enrolment baseline): Even a 1-3 percentage-point VSL conversion lift on the Astro Flipping enrolment surface, at current podcast and YouTube traffic, sits in the low-to-mid six-figure incremental enrolment range. The scorecard turns currently-discarded mid-funnel traffic into segmented email, which compounds across cycles. The application funnel on Housing Alchemy and Energy of Money replaces manual Work With Us back-and-forth with automated qualified booking. The unified hub compounds the cross-brand cross-sell that does not happen today. Results vary, dependent on operator inputs, existing enrolment baseline, and audience response.
